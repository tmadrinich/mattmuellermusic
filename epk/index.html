The Artist

Original Songs, Played Loud

Matt Mueller is a country songwriter from southern Minnesota, raised on a small hobby farm and writing about the life he knows. His songs are personal and heartfelt, and his shows are built to be felt.

A 2025 Midwest Country Music Organization Song of the Year nominee, Matt has played the Minnesota State Fair and the Steele County Fair, and since 2023 he has co-led a high-energy band across 25+ stages in Minnesota and Iowa, from Mower County Fair to Czech Days. He knows how to hold a festival crowd, and he brings direct tour support experience.

His latest single, “Queen of Attention,” is out now everywhere you stream.
